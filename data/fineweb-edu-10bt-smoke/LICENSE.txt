FineWeb-Edu smoke subset: license and attribution

This directory contains tokenized, truncated data derived from the
sample-10BT subset of FineWeb-Edu, released by Hugging Face.

Upstream attribution:
Anton Lozhkov, Loubna Ben Allal, Leandro von Werra, and Thomas Wolf (2024).
FineWeb-Edu: the Finest Collection of Educational Content. Hugging Face.
Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-edu

Terms of Use:
https://commoncrawl.org/terms-of-use

Local preparation:
The source data was tokenized using Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B-Base and stored as
uint32 token IDs. Prefixes of the existing train.bin and test.bin were
retained to create this smoke subset:
- train.bin: 5,241,600 tokens (20,966,400 bytes).
- test.bin: 524,160 tokens (2,096,640 bytes).
